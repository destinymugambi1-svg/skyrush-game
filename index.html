How to play

Choose a virtual bet amount and press BET before the countdown ends. During a flight, press CASH OUT before the crash to receive virtual credits based on the displayed multiplier. If the round crashes first, the active virtual bet is lost. Results are simulated locally and do not predict real-world games.
